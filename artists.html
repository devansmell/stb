TaLLLon

a young, energetic four-piece alternative rock band from galway in the west of ireland. the band first connected in the summer of 2023 and have since been developing their sound, live performance and identity, quickly making themselves known across ireland and the uk.

loud and calculated: a collision of energetic, melodic alternative rock, post-grunge and hard rock, known for anthemic choruses, powerful guitar riffs that blend with catchy hooks, frontman liam's uplifting, raw vocals and lyrics rooted in punk underground energy.
